# Capital Aperture Persona UAT Matrix

Status: owner-scoped paper UAT only. No real-money rail. No shared or fabricated production credentials.

## Access contract

- Local simulation identities must use the isolated UAT database and owner-scoped fixtures.
- Production UAT uses individually invited OAuth accounts. Credentials are never stored in Git or shared between personas.
- A `uat_identity` query parameter is a fixture label, not production impersonation.
- Every journey stops before paper approval or submission unless the owner confirms the exact ticket at action time.

## Shared thesis

Football-season engagement may lift public betting-platform activity, while new state regulation may restrict market access or change the eligible product mix. The bullish demand mechanism and regulatory headwind must be evaluated separately. A named catalyst is declared evidence, not verified evidence.

## Persona simulations

### 1. Intraday Operator

- Mission: find a same-session shares-only expression after a named regulation or company catalyst.
- Needs: fresh tape, VWAP/range confirmation, volume, liquidity, exact stop distance, flat-by time.
- Valid outcomes: one bounded paper ticket, conditional hold, or preserve cash.
- Fail if: another operator's thesis appears, catalyst is lost, stale data looks actionable, or no next action exists.

### 2. Defined-Risk Options Operator

- Mission: compare a multi-week long call, long put, and cash without exceeding premium-at-risk.
- Needs: exact OCC contract, expiry, strike, bid/ask/mid, spread, volume, open interest, IV, DTE, breakeven, entitlement, quote time, and catalyst-to-expiry fit.
- Valid outcomes: verified single-leg paper ticket, watch for chain evidence, or preserve cash.
- Fail if: equity valuation checks unlock an option proposal, contract liquidity is unknown, or premium math cannot be reconstructed.

### 3. Cash Steward

- Mission: preserve optionality when evidence, liquidity, or portfolio headroom does not clear.
- Needs: $0-risk receipt, blocker, structured reopen condition, look-back date, and retained best alternative.
- Valid outcomes: scheduled look-back followed by cash remained correct, too early, too conservative, or inconclusive.
- Fail if: the receipt disappears, cannot be resolved, or cash is omitted from the learning record.

### 4. Catalyst Operator

- Mission: test the football-season demand mechanism against a specific state-regulation event.
- Needs: primary-source event, effective date, affected states/products, directly exposed issuers, contradictory evidence, and explicit invalidation.
- Valid outcomes: evidence-qualified research, named conditional gate, or preserve cash.
- Fail if: a declared catalyst is shown as absent, a declaration is shown as verified, or indirect proxies outrank direct expressions without explanation.

### 5. Portfolio Operator

- Mission: compare DKNG, FLUT, PENN, indirect exposure, and cash without duplicating current holdings or breaching concentration.
- Needs: current positions, account freshness, single-name/sector/theme headroom, correlation, aggregate planned loss, and a portfolio-gap explanation.
- Valid outcomes: one portfolio-compatible paper proposal, conditional hold, or preserve cash.
- Fail if: concentration is only a warning, duplicate exposure is hidden, or multiple cards compete for the primary action.

## Cross-persona acceptance path

1. Start from a clean owner-scoped thesis choice.
2. Choose the play expression for this run without rewriting the canonical thesis.
3. Normalize catalyst, horizon, instrument, capital, loss ceiling, and invalidation.
4. Rank a compressed Play Slate with cash always available.
5. Open optional evidence depth; never require reading every candidate.
6. Prepare an exact paper proposal only after all instrument-specific evidence passes.
7. Keep approval and submission separate.
8. Operate from Play Desk: research, paper tickets, in motion, and outcomes.
9. Record the horizon result and preserve the immutable original decision.
