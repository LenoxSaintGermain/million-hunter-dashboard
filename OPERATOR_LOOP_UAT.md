# Compact Operator Loop — Browser UAT

## Command Center

The former static status headline has been replaced by a compact **Your operator pulse**. It shows active targets, current tailwind count, four compact operating metrics, and direct actions to Research Journeys or a scan. The fixed “AGGRESSIVE” display is absent.

## Memo Triage

The Memo Library presents validated and needs-review counts as direct filters. Selecting LLY expands its decision context **in the same page**—including source-backed thesis fit and direct actions to decisive checks or the complete research record. No intermediate search page is required.

## Paper Readiness

The paper-readiness view presents a four-step research-to-action loop: synchronize context, review decisive checks, approve a paper order, then monitor paper fills and follow-up. The Alpaca paper account is explicitly labeled **Connected** and auto-synced; the imported holdings account is explicitly labeled **Manual context**, with no broker synchronization or order submission available.
