```text
SIGNAL HUNTER — BEFORE YOU COMMIT
Approved factual boundaries for a lobby orientation draft, October 1, 2026.

Signal Hunter is framed as an evidence-first deal diligence and decision desk for buyers, acquisition entrepreneurs, independent sponsors and investment teams, including family-office teams. Search is one entrypoint, not the product identity. A broker introduction, an existing target or a business idea can start the questions.

The work is to distinguish seller claims, modeled assumptions, supporting evidence and unresolved questions before committing capital. Research informs a decision; a human authorizes the next action. Source links and agreement among agents do not independently verify a claim.

The public illustrative walkthrough demonstrates a composite acquisition case. It is not a real customer, recommendation or observed investment outcome. Its reported seller's discretionary earnings (SDE) are $720,000. A sensitivity case removes $223,000 of disputed add-backs, leaving $497,000 before other adjustments. This is subtraction under an assumption, not audited or professionally normalized earnings. The user should ask which add-backs can be supported, how customer relationships transfer and what replacing the owner's work would cost. No savings, returns or financing approval are established.

Strategic questions may extend beyond the advertised business: does property ownership change the case, would this be a useful add-on, or does owner dependence undermine the economics? These are investigation hypotheses, not discovered facts. Property, business acquisition and venture scenarios need compatible evidence and rules. Tax questions belong with qualified advisers; no tax outcome is promised.

Document-first intake is the intended next journey: bring a CIM, financial statements and contracts, review extracted claims and references, challenge assumptions, and prepare a decision with open questions intact. Secure document upload and end-to-end ingestion are not established as production-ready by the release evidence reviewed for this film. Any depiction of upload or extraction must be prominently labeled ROADMAP PREVIEW. Do not invite viewers to upload confidential documents today.

The acquisition V2 engine has local implementation/prototype evidence, but live-search acceptance remains pending in the reviewed release records. Do not advertise V2 as fully live or proven end to end. The public examples are frozen, illustrative experiences, not live analysis. Current Capital research UAT also has an unresolved classifier-contract failure; do not promise universal reliability or production readiness. Capital execution is practice trading only, with separate human approval and risk gates.

Signal Hunter sits upstream of Quality of Earnings and professional accounting, legal, tax and lender diligence. It does not replace them. No automatic acquisition, order, approval or guaranteed outcome is claimed.

An optional secondary example, Jim's file, is a released illustrative Cybercab venture scenario with fictional assumptions and unresolved evidence requirements. It is not a vehicle availability claim, permit, endorsement, commercial forecast or executed business. It demonstrates a question: what must be true for a business idea to work? It is optional and not needed in the main acquisition film.

The next step offered in this orientation is: Work through a case. The public walkthrough uses illustrative data. It does not process a visitor's documents or run live agents.
```
