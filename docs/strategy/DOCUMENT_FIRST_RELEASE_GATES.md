# Document-first decision desk — integration and release gates

Status: local implementation and research in progress. Not a production release receipt.

## Positioning contract

Bring a deal and its evidence. Understand what the claims support, what remains unknown, and what would change the decision before committing capital. Search is one input channel; uploaded materials, broker material and operator research are other intended channels. This is upstream of professional financial, legal and tax diligence, not a replacement or an investment approval.

The decision experience must serve acquisition entrepreneurs, repeat buyers, sponsors and investment teams without implying that every asset uses the same rules. Alternative property or strategic angles are hypotheses requiring evidence and operator approval, never invented findings.

## Before private document intake can be called ready

1. Authenticate and scope every document, job, result and download to its owner; test cross-owner denial.
2. Validate file type, size and signature; quarantine/scan supported formats. Declare unsupported, encrypted and scanned documents rather than silently extracting nothing.
3. Disclose storage, retention/deletion and provider processing before submission. Keep secrets server-side. Do not place confidential documents in public fixtures, logs or links.
4. Preserve source bytes/hash and extraction version; carry page/span provenance into proposed facts. Document instructions remain untrusted data.
5. Present claims, assumptions, contradictions and unknowns for correction. A confirmed mapping is not independent verification.
6. Require an explicitly confirmed compatible mandate. Fleet/property/scenario analyses must not silently inherit business-acquisition gates.
7. Use deterministic engine logic for numbers and gates; retain source-bound research questions and audit formulas. Missing does not equal zero or pass.
8. Bind every evaluation to document, extraction, fact-review and mandate revisions. Any relevant edit invalidates approval and downstream results.
9. Render the same evidence snapshot in the editorial report, with a next action for every unresolved item and progressive disclosure on mobile.
10. Exercise synthetic upload-to-review-to-evaluation UAT first, then authorized private-document UAT with deletion/read-back checks. Build success is not end-to-end acceptance.

## Public/demo gate

Public demos remain deterministic, zero-API, anonymous and explicitly illustrative. They may demonstrate the intended workflow with frozen fixtures; they must not say a file was processed if it was not. New pricing remains a proposal until approved and reconciled with billing entitlements. No live rollout is recorded by this document.
